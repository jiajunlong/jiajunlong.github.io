About me

My research focuses on long-context modeling and multimodal large language models.

I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Artificial Intelligence at Beihang University. I am interested in making language and vision-language models more effective and efficient through better data, training methods, and evaluation. My work spans long-context data synthesis, context extension, and small-scale multimodal models.
